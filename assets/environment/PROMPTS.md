# Environment generation prompts

These original Cinderwake assets were generated with the built-in ImageGen tool. Source PNGs are preserved as generated; the engine crops atlas regions and uses nearest-neighbor sampling. Biome plates are opaque. Terrain and props use transparency.

## terrain-v1.png

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: transparent environment sprite atlas for Cinderwake, a 2D side-scrolling action roguelite with finely detailed brass-armored and teal-cloaked sprite characters.
1536x1024 canvas, strict 4 columns x 4 rows. Each cell 384x256, one isolated environment sprite per cell with 25 pixels transparent gutter all around. REAL transparent alpha background. No text, no grid lines, no characters, no background, no ground shadows. Do not overlap adjacent cells.
Style: premium detailed pixel art, small irregular pixel clusters and 3-4 stepped hue-shifted shading tones, subtle irregular chips and wear, carved material details, crisp selective outlines. Matches richly shaded modern roguelite sprites. NO flat chunky rectangles, no simple vector shapes, no blur, no smooth gradients.
Every row corresponds to a biome and has four assets:
COLUMN 1: a floating platform, a horizontally level straight playable top edge, approximately 310px wide and 60px thick, detailed stone/copper slab with decorative brackets, ivy or chains hanging downward. Keep the top surface uninterrupted; all depth extends below it.
COLUMN 2: a ground ledge module, approximately 330px wide and 115px deep, straight flat top surface and layered stone/metal cross-section below, jagged chipped lower outline. Left/right sides meet cleanly for horizontal repeats. No perspective, exact side view.
COLUMN 3: one connected vertical decorative silhouette/clump, roughly 130px wide and 190px tall, transparent around it.
COLUMN 4: one small environmental prop cluster, roughly 130px wide and 140px tall, fully connected and resting on a consistent bottom baseline.
ROW 1 AQUEDUCT: dark indigo carved stone, tarnished copper reinforcing plates, restrained mint rim highlights, small green moss/ivy. Column3 a hanging copper pipe with connected ivy; column4 a brass lantern mounted on a weathered stone pedestal.
ROW 2 CONSERVATORY: aged marble/brass with fine rust-orange leaves and dark crimson vines. Column3 a slender twisted tree branch with connected orange foliage; column4 an overgrown ornamental urn.
ROW 3 FOUNDRY: soot-dark wrought steel, copper rivets, small ember-orange seams and chains. Column3 an oxidized vertical industrial pipe; column4 a small ornate furnace brazier.
ROW 4 CROWN: intricately carved charcoal stone with antique gold inlays, muted teal details. Column3 a slim carved gothic column; column4 a small broken brass astronomical instrument on a pedestal.
Constraints: all 16 complete isolated sprites fit within their cells. Source art, not a game screenshot or concept-board mockup. Platform and ground TOP SURFACES MUST BE FLAT and clearly readable.

Each object completely inside its cell with at least 35px empty padding; side-view orthographic; consistent antique copper, charcoal stone and teal glass palette.
